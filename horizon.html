▸

Simplifications for v1: purchase costs are a flat estimate rather than banded stamp duty; growth and amortisation are calculated annually rather than monthly; no tax, ISA/pension allowance, or income-tax modelling. Every figure is calculated in nominal (future) pounds internally — that's the only way compounding stays correct — and only converted to today's money at display time when "Real" is selected above, using the inflation rate below. Enough to compare shapes of futures, not a substitute for a mortgage advisor, pensions administrator, or independent financial advice.

Drives the allocation table on each scenario below, and the retirement-age lever further down. Defaults to what's actually unallocated in Cash flow & saving — raise it to explore a hypothetical raise, bonus, or side income.

Each scenario now shows guaranteed pension income, estimated DC income, and net worth as three separate figures — deliberately never summed into one number, since a guaranteed income stream and a savings pot aren't the same thing. One honest limitation: contributions are modelled as continuing at the same rate right through to 90, not stopping or changing at retirement — so "net worth at 90" shows what happens if saving carries on as-is, not what happens if you start drawing it down.

Retirement security

Will your income actually cover a modest, non-lavish life — housing, utilities, the odd holiday — not whether you're "rich." Income here is pension income plus a drawdown estimate from whatever savings and deposit pot you've accumulated by retirement (same 4% rule as DC pots) — savings are a retirement plan too, not just a separate pot. Always shown in today's money. Target based on Pensions UK's published Retirement Living Standards (2026/27, two-person household, excludes housing).

Exact figure is editable in Assumptions ("Target living cost") if none of these three fit.

Checkpoints

Save a dated snapshot of where things stand — income, outgoings, savings, deposit, and pensions — so you can look back later and see what's actually changed. Comparing two checkpoints against each other comes in a future release; for now, save and review.

Horizon is a scenario-comparison tool for household planning conversations, not financial advice. Projections depend entirely on the assumptions you set above and are illustrative, not a forecast or a recommendation to buy, rent, or invest in any particular way. Pension figures are estimates built from what you enter — check accrued defined benefit amounts against your latest annual benefit statement, and your own State Pension forecast at gov.uk, rather than relying on this app's defaults.
