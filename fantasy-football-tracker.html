External team news check

This app has no live source for team news or predicted lineups — FPL's API doesn't expose it, and the free websites that do don't offer a public API. The quickest way in is the Get External Review button on the Advice tab; the tools here (generate/copy/import) still work standalone too. Run the prompt in a fresh chat (Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini — whichever you use), let it search the web for current team news on your flagged players and give its own starting-XI opinion AND a hold-or-transfer verdict (using your saved free transfer count and bank), then paste its JSON reply back in to import it here. Genuinely informs the Advice tab's starting-XI, captain, and transfer choices where a confident signal exists — see the "External advice considered" note there for exactly what, if anything, was adjusted.
